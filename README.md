# Hi, I'm Saj.
 
I started by knocking on doors for environmental justice campaigns. Now I run a national data program by day and build tools by night. The arc from organizer to data practitioner to builder shapes everything I make.
 
I build civic tech, consumer apps, and tools I needed and couldn't find. The through-line is the same: start with the specific person, not the category.
 
---
 
## What I'm building
 
**[CivicRadar](https://civicradar.co)** — Live. An identity-first civic action app. It matches you to state and federal legislation based on who you are, not which issue silo you already follow, then helps you act on it. Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind, Claude Haiku. OpenStates, LegiScan, and Congress.gov. AGPL v3.
 
**[Euphora](https://euphora.app)** — Live. A self-guided speech and voice practice app across five lanes, from stuttering to confidence to gender-affirming voice work. It refuses to score fluency with AI, because that is the wrong tool for the job. Local-first, grounded in evidence-tiered speech science.
 
**Untitled taste project** — In development. A cross-media taste portrait for movies, TV, games, and books, built on your own words and verdicts instead of collaborative filtering.
